# Counting Steiner designs with prescribed color profiles

Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini · *Combinatorial entropy and counting*, Paper III · Preprint 1.0, 24 November 2026.

For a fixed vertex coloring, Theorem 1.1 counts labeled Steiner 2-designs with a prescribed interior integer histogram. Corollary 10.1 gives the large deviations of the histogram of a uniform design. Figure 1 illustrates the open-set lower bound at a boundary point.
