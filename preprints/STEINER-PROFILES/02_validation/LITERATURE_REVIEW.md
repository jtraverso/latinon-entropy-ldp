# Literature review for Paper III

Reviewed 24 November 2026. Scope: labeled pair designs of fixed block size on a fixed vertex coloring, exact integer type quotas, and the induced profile large deviations. The starting bibliography was Paper II, especially references 1, 6–10, 19, 21, 24 and 30. Searches of indexed primary papers used “counting designs prescribed colours”, “Steiner systems prescribed block types”, “counting decompositions prescribed numbers of cliques”, “random greedy matching” and variants with “entropy”, “partition”, and “large deviations”. The alphaXiv search also retrieved the author's [Paper II](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.steiner-latin-squares-color-statistics).

| Work | Comparison used in the manuscript |
|---|---|
| [Paper I](https://github.com/jtraverso/latinon-entropy-ldp) and [Paper II](https://github.com/jtraverso/color-statistics-steiner-latin) | Paper I counts Latin squares in neighborhoods. Paper II, Theorem 1.5 and Corollary 1.6, is the direct triple-system predecessor for the unrestricted set of color types. |
| [Keevash, Counting designs, Theorem 6.1](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/keevash/papers/count-designs.pdf) | General unrestricted enumeration specializes to the one-color case. It already extends the entropy upper argument beyond triples. |
| [Linial and Luria](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5042), [Bennett and Bohman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.3581) | Entropy revelation for triples and tracking a random greedy matching are antecedents of different parts of the proof. |
| [Keevash, Coloured and directed designs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05770) and [The existence of designs II](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.05900) | Partite and colored decomposition frameworks are relevant to existence and general counting methods. A deduction of this uniform bilateral error for vertex-type quotas from the general framework was not located. |
| [Kwan, Sah and Sawhney, §2.2](https://mkwn.github.io/EMLS.pdf) | Prescribed clique counts in a complete-graph decomposition are an especially close comparison. The statistic there is the number of cliques of each size. Paper III fixes the size and varies the vertex-color multiset. |
| [Delcourt and Postle, Theorem 1.7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17855) | Direct input to the exact covering, attributed where it enters in Section 6. |
| [Ehard, Glock and Joos](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.09946), [Simkin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13460) | Related weighted matching and entropy-enumeration methods in the Paper II bibliography. Neither is invoked as a lemma in the present argument. |

**Assessment.** The sources examined support the comparisons in §1.3. The search did not locate the stated exact interior fiber estimate with both displayed power savings and the same vertex-type statistic for general fixed block size. This is a scoped search result. The manuscript makes the specific comparisons above and asserts no exhaustive priority claim.

The graph specialization used in Lemma 6.1 was also checked against the [original Theorem 1.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.17855v1) and its locally frozen adapter.
